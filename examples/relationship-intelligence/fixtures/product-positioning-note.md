# Product Positioning Note

Marshmallow's relationship-intelligence wedge is not a CRM replacement. The
promise is that an agent can prepare a person for a meeting by loading a small,
source-backed model of the relationship.

The first demo should prove five primitives:

1. relationship timeline
2. relationship state
3. pre-meeting brief
4. post-meeting capture
5. next thoughtful action

For the investor story, lead with "never walk into a relationship cold again."
Avoid generic memory, broad company brain claims, and hidden capture. The trust
advantage is local-first, explicit, Markdown-native context.
