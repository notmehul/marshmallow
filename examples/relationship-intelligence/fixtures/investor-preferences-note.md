# Investor Preferences Note

Rowan likes brief, sourced material before a meeting. He does not want a CRM
dump, a biography, or a transcript summary. He wants to know:

- why the meeting matters now
- what changed since the last interaction
- the relationship state and any trust debt
- open loops, promises, and asks
- the next thoughtful action
- which source backs each claim

He reacts badly to generic "company brain" language. He prefers language that
shows private, inspectable context: source cards, graph nodes, compact briefs,
and explicit limits.
