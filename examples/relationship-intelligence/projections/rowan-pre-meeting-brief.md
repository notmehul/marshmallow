# Rowan Pre-Meeting Relationship Brief

## Use For

Prepare the fictional founder Naya for a June 27, 2026 investor meeting with
the fictional investor Rowan. This packet is dummy data for a public demo. It
shows how Marshmallow can help someone walk into a relationship-sensitive
meeting with source-backed context.

## Load First

- [[rowan-naya-relationship]] - relationship state and open loop.
- [[rowan-investor]] - what Rowan wants before a meeting.
- [[pre-meeting-brief-flow]] - expected shape of the brief.
- [[marshmallow-relationship-wedge]] - product narrative to prove.

## Brief

**Why now:** Rowan asked for the next demo to feel people-first: less
infrastructure, more meeting preparedness. Naya promised to send a polished demo
before the June 27 meeting.

**What changed:** The pitch moved from broad "AI memory" to relationship state
over time: what changed, what was promised, who trusts whom, and the next
thoughtful action.

**Relationship state:** Warm but conditional. Rowan trusts the visible source
trail and is interested in the wedge, but he wants proof that the brief changes
meeting behavior.

**Open loop:** Can Marshmallow produce a compact source-backed brief that is
more useful than a CRM entry or transcript summary?

**Meeting ask:** Ask whether this brief is strong enough to earn the two
operator-investor introductions Rowan offered to make after a successful demo.

**Next thoughtful action:** Send the brief with the source trail visible, then
capture Rowan's feedback as a new source card only if he explicitly provides
material worth learning.

- `investor-preferences-note`
- `product-positioning-note`
- `last-meeting-note`
