# Rowan Naya Relationship

The relationship is warm but still conditional. Rowan has moved from skeptical
of broad AI-memory framing to interested in a specific people-first workflow.
He trusts the source trail, but the next proof must show practical meeting
preparedness rather than infrastructure.

## Evidence

- `relationship-timeline-note` - Rowan pushed the pitch toward relationship

- `investor-preferences-note` - he wants why now, what changed, trust state,
  open loops, asks, and source backing.

  next demo proves a grounded relationship brief.

## Use In Work

- Name the state as warm but conditional.
- Treat the promised demo as the main open loop.

introductions after a successful demo.
