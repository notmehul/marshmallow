# Rowan Investor

## Current Model

Rowan is a fictional investor persona for the public demo. He wants concise
material that helps him enter a meeting prepared: why the meeting matters now,
what changed, relationship state, trust debt, open loops, asks, and the next
thoughtful action.

## Evidence

- `investor-preferences-note` - Rowan wants sourced material before a meeting
  and dislikes CRM dumps, transcript summaries, generic company-brain language,
  and unsourced claims.
- `last-meeting-note` - he found the visible source trail credible and asked
  for a demo that feels people-first.

## Use In Work

- Keep briefs short and source-backed.
- Mention explicit limits when evidence is thin.
- Avoid broad "AI memory" or "company brain" positioning.

## Limits

This node is fictional and exists only to demonstrate relationship-intelligence
behavior.
