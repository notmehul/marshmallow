# Marshmallow Relationship Wedge

## Current Model

The demo should make the relationship-intelligence wedge tangible. Marshmallow
is not claiming to replace a CRM. It is showing that source-backed relationship
state can make a person better prepared before a meeting.

## Evidence

- `product-positioning-note` - the wedge is source-backed relationship state
  for meeting preparation, not a CRM replacement.
- `relationship-timeline-note` - Rowan rejected broad AI-memory language and
  pushed toward relationship state over time.

## Use In Work

- Lead with "never walk into a relationship cold again."
- Show the five primitives: relationship timeline, relationship state,
  pre-meeting brief, post-meeting capture, and next thoughtful action.
- Keep the proof grounded in source cards, graph nodes, indexes, and
  projections.

## Limits

Do not imply background capture, hidden learning, or a hosted contact database.
