# Pre Meeting Brief Flow

## Current Model

The brief is a runtime aid. It should condense the few graph nodes that matter
into a usable pre-meeting view: why now, what changed, relationship state,
trust debt, open loops, asks, source trail, and the next thoughtful action.

## Evidence

- `investor-preferences-note` - Rowan's expected fields are explicit.
- `product-positioning-note` - the demo should prove the pre-meeting brief and
  next thoughtful action primitives.
- `last-meeting-note` - the next demo should feel like a person walking into a
  meeting prepared.

## Use In Work

- Produce a brief, not a biography.
- Include a source trail for claims that affect judgment.
- Close with the next thoughtful action and the meeting ask.

## Limits

Do not turn this into deterministic generation. Agents use the recalled packet
and graph nodes to draft the brief.
