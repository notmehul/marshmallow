# Retention Proof Plan

For the next six weeks, prioritize repeat usage from design partners over
fundraising momentum. Keep investor communication direct about that choice.

The revisit condition is four consecutive weekly cohorts above the repeat-usage
target. Until then, the next useful work is retention evidence, not a growth-round
narrative. When the condition is met—or the runway premise changes—update this
plan and the connected company, decision, and relationship state.
