# Commuter Rush Cutoff Rule

## Current Model

**P7-RAMP** accessibility ramp installation on **Pier 7** cannot block **commuter rush** morning boarding before the **09:30 cutoff**. Contractors must clear the Pier 7 approach by 09:15 so passengers can board on schedule. Marcus Holt and Elena Crane reinforced this window at the 2026-05-15 Friday Harbor diversion huddle when ramp work forced the Friday Harbor run to Pier 5. The cutoff applies on weekdays and weekends when ramp or noise work threatens the morning rush.

## Evidence

- `2026-05-15-friday-harbor-diversion` - **Pier 7 ramp** work cannot block **commuter rush** boarding before **09:30 cutoff** with contractors clearing the approach by 09:15; **Friday Harbor run** moves to **Pier 5** boarding for **2026-05-15** and through weekend if noise window slips; Marcus directed to publish pier 5 diversion notice to terminal boards by 0700.

## Use In Work

- Block P7-RAMP contractor staging and impact-tool work in the Pier 7 approach corridor between 06:00 and 09:30 on commuter sail days unless the berth is fully cleared for boarding.
- Coordinate with Marcus Holt before scheduling ramp tasks that overlap the morning rush window — divert affected sailings rather than delay boarding past 09:30.

## Limits

The 09:30 cutoff governs passenger boarding access only — not Surfside Condos quiet hours (no impact tools before 0800 weekdays / 0900 weekends) or Friday Harbor diversion pier assignments. P9-GATE critical path work on Pier 9 is unrelated.
