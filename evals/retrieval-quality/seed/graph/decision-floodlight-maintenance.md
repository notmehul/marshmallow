# Floodlight Maintenance Decision

## Current Model

Cal Donner switched Pier 9 **floodlight circuit** B-7 to **maintenance mode** on 2026-06-03 for P9-GATE **gate commissioning** while isolating west gate fixtures from normal night operations. Gangway lane sodium fixtures stayed energized so Bridgeway Hoist crew could continue handrail touch-ups on the P9-GANG apron. Victor Dunn confirmed lane sensors polling with no bleed into the gangway apron. Cal logged the entry in the night harbormaster book: "P9 west gate lights isolated, gangway lit."
