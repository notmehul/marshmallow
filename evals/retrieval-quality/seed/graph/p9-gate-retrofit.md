# P9-Gate Retrofit

## Current Model

The **Pier-9 Gate Retrofit** project tracks under codename **P9-GATE** and covers fare lane automation, gate automation, and night lighting circuit upgrades at Pier 9. Introduced at Q2 kickoff still under the Kelwick single-contractor envelope, the **gate retrofit** scope split to Turnstile Dynamics after Victor Dunn superseded unified contracting on 2026-05-06. Gangway staging on P9-GANG remains on a separate calendar.
