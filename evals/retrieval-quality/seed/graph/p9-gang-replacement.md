# P9-Gang Replacement

## Current Model

The **Pier-9 Gangway Replacement** project tracks under codename **P9-GANG** and covers the hydraulic gangway module, load test hooks, and hose spec at Pier 9. Q2 kickoff introduced **gangway replacement** scope overlapping gate staging vocabulary but under its own codename in the terminal readiness playbook. Victor Dunn inspected the **mock-up gangway** on 2026-05-20 and confirmed **gangway width** at **2.4 meters** within tolerance on Bridgeway drawing rev C, with SAE 100R2AT hose verified on tag. Priya Nandakumar previewed load test certificate paperwork — passenger use remains blocked until signed.

- `2026-04-20-q2-kickoff-terminal` - Kickoff notes list **Pier-9 Gangway Replacement** as **P9-GANG** (hydraulic gangway, staging overlap with gate side) under the Kelwick Marine single-contractor envelope pending procurement split.
- `2026-04-24-pier9-dual-scope-intro` - Victor Dunn's dual-scope session separates **Pier-9 Gangway Replacement** (hydraulic gangway module, load test hooks, hose spec TBD) from gate work; **P9-GANG** codename is live in the playbook tracker.
