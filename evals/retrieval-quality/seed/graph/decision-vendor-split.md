# Vendor Split Decision

## Current Model

Derek Blunt's 2026-04-27 email formally split Pier 9 procurement into two vendor tracks. **Turnstile Dynamics** is the **p9-gate vendor** and **gate vendor** for P9-GATE — gate automation, fare lane hardware, and commissioning support. **Bridgeway Hoist** is the **p9-gang vendor** and **gangway vendor** for P9-GANG — gangway fabrication and hydraulic install for the **gangway replacement** scope. Kelwick Marine remained on old pier 9 single-contractor paperwork until Victor Dunn superseded it on 2026-05-06; Glenn Wexler noted two PO tracks mean two change-order caps.

## Evidence

- `2026-04-27-vendor-split-announcement` - Derek Blunt assigns **P9-GATE → Turnstile Dynamics** as **p9-gate vendor** for gate automation and fare lane hardware; **P9-GANG → Bridgeway Hoist** as **p9-gang vendor** owning **gangway replacement** scope; Kelwick stays on old pier 9 single-contractor paperwork until Victor supersedes it.

## Use In Work

- Route P9-GATE quotes, badges, and delivery slips to Turnstile Dynamics — never to Bridgeway Hoist or superseded Kelwick Marine.
- Route P9-GANG gangway fabrication, hydraulic install, and mobilization discounts to Bridgeway Hoist as the gangway vendor.

## Limits

This decision assigns vendors only — it does not set change-order caps ($18,400 on P9-GATE per Glenn), delivery slip dates (gate May 28 vs gangway May 21), or hydraulic hose specs (SAE 100R2AT on P9-GANG). Kelwick quoting ceased after formal supersession on 2026-05-06.
