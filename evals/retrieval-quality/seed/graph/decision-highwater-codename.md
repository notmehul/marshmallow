# Highwater Codename Decision

## Current Model

**SSB-DRILL** uses codename "Highwater" in **radio traffic** during simulation weekends — the official **Highwater codename** for open-channel communications with no project numbers spoken on air. Priya Nandakumar established the call sign at Highwater prep on 2026-05-25 alongside confirmation of **three simulation weekends** for the storm surge barrier drill series. Cal Donner's night logs later recorded clean Highwater radio traffic through the final simulation weekend.

## Evidence

- `2026-05-25-highwater-drill-prep` - Priya Nandakumar walkthrough locks official **Highwater codename** for **radio traffic** during **ssb-drill** events with no project numbers on open channels; **three simulation weekends** confirmed — May 24 (done), May 31, Jun 7 — with life-vest protocol at 45-minute spot check intervals per packet.

## Use In Work

- Use "Highwater" on radio channels during SSB-DRILL simulation weekends rather than project codenames, pier numbers, or vendor names.
- When retrieving drill communications artifacts, filter by highwater codename and ssb-drill context to avoid conflating with P9-GATE or P9-GANG pier retrofit traffic.

## Limits

Codename governs open-channel radio traffic only — written artifacts, badge batches, and procurement documents still use SSB-DRILL or Storm Surge Barrier Drill Series identifiers. Pier 9 infrastructure codenames (P9-GATE, P9-GANG) are unrelated.
