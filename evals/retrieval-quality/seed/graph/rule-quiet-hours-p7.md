# Quiet Hours P7 Rule

## Current Model

**Elena Crane** escalated a **noise complaint** from **Surfside Condos** about early-morning **pier 7 ramp** grinding on **2026-05-27**. Building representatives attached decibel readings and threatened city permit review. Juno Castillo scheduled **quiet hours** enforcement starting the next day: no impact tools before 0800 weekdays and 0900 weekends on P7-RAMP work. The commuter **09:30 cutoff** for blocking morning boarding still applies independently of quiet hours.

## Use In Work

- Block P7-RAMP impact-tool work (grinding, cutting, pile driving) before 0800 weekdays and 0900 weekends following the Surfside escalation — log exceptions through Elena Crane.

## Limits

Quiet hours govern noise from impact tools only — not gangway load tests on Pier 9, Pier 9 floodlight maintenance mode, or SSB-DRILL deckhand rostering. Surfside complaint did not change Friday Harbor diversion pier assignments.
