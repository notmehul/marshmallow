# Pier 9 Single-Contractor Plan

## Current Model

The **Pier 9 Single-Contractor Plan** was Victor Dunn's unified Pier 9 contracting envelope naming **Kelwick Marine** for all gate and gangway work under one mobilization, shared badge pool, and single liability rider. The **single-contractor plan** covered both **gate retrofit** and **gangway replacement** at Q2 kickoff while P9-GATE and P9-GANG codenames went live in the **terminal readiness playbook** tracker. Victor Dunn formally retired the **pier 9 single-contractor** track on **2026-05-06** citing **scope divergence** — gate automation and gangway hydraulic specs could not share one Kelwick envelope.

**Status:** Inactive — **superseded single-contractor** execution moved to split vendor tracks under the Q2 Terminal Readiness Playbook: P9-GATE / Turnstile Dynamics and P9-GANG / Bridgeway Hoist. Kelwick Marine remains on the old plan for audit trail only.
