# Supersede Single-Contractor Decision

## Current Model

Victor Dunn formally retired the unified Pier 9 contracting envelope on **2026-05-06**. His slide title cited **scope divergence** — gate automation (Turnstile Dynamics) and gangway replacement (Bridgeway Hoist) diverged in schedule, liability, and hydraulic specs so one Kelwick Marine envelope could not cover both. The **superseded single-contractor** track moved to split vendor execution under the Q2 Terminal Readiness Playbook: P9-GATE / Turnstile Dynamics and P9-GANG / Bridgeway Hoist. Kelwick Marine remains named on the old pier 9 single-contractor plan for audit trail only.
